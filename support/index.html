Common questions

Privora shows no apps after an analysis

Some phones, mostly from vivo, iQOO, Xiaomi, OPPO and other makers, hide the app list until you allow Read installed apps. Tap Analyze now and allow it when Android asks. If you declined earlier, open Android Settings → Apps → Privora → Permissions and allow it there.

Does Privora remove, block or change apps?

No. Privora only reads and explains. When you want to change something, it opens the right page in Android's own Settings, and you decide there.

Does Privora need the internet?

No. Privora has no internet permission. Everything happens on your phone. See the privacy policy.

What does the score mean?

It is a rule-based summary of the access that the apps Privora can see hold right now. It describes what apps can access, not what they do with it, and it can't prove that a phone is free of problems.

Privora Watch stopped sending alerts

Android may pause apps that you haven't opened for a while, and some phone makers limit background work. Open Privora to check when Watch last ran. In Android Settings → Apps → Privora, turn off Pause app activity if unused and allow background activity or autostart if your phone offers it.

How do I delete my data?

In Privora, open Settings → Clear local analysis history. Uninstalling Privora also deletes all of its data.
